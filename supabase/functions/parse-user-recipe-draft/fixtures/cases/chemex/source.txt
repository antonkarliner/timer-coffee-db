We're gonna keep our dose at 60 grams per liter, so for this brew that's 30 grams of coffee to 500 grams of water. Quick aside on filters -- a Chemex filter is noticeably heavier than a normal V60 filter, it actually weighs about 4.5 grams dry versus around 1.5 grams for a Hario paper, which is part of why the Chemex cup comes out so clean. Fold the filter, rinse it with hot water, and dose in your coffee. In terms of the bloom, do exactly as the V60: 2 to 1 to 3 to 1 in terms of bloom water to coffee dose, so 60 to 90 grams of bloom water, and let that bloom for at least 45 seconds. Once the bloom settles, start pouring in slow concentric circles, pretty controlled, maybe 3 grams a second, avoiding the filter walls, until you reach the full 500 grams. You're gonna have four to five minute brew times brewing half a liter with the Chemex, and you're gonna have great tasting coffee. At the end, give it a little stir and a swirl to help the drawdown and keep a flat bed, then just let it finish draining completely.
